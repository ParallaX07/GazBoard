## What's Changed

**Made with GazBoard:** every exported PDF page, SVG and PNG carries a small grey "Made with GazBoard" in its bottom-right corner, clear of your work. In a PDF or an SVG, GazBoard is a link to the GazBoard project page.

**PDFs carry the board's name:** Adobe Reader and other PDF readers now show the board's name as the document title, instead of "sheet.html".
