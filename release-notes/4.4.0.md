## What's Changed

**Arrows that hold on:** hover over a shape, note, text, picture or group and pull an arrow out of one of its blue dots, or draw with the Arrow tool from one thing to another. The arrow stays joined when you move things, and its ends slide round to the nearest side.

**Straight, elbow or curved:** pick from the selection bar at any time. Drag the middle of a curved arrow to bend it, or drag an end onto something else to reconnect it.

**Release notes on Android** now show as proper paragraphs instead of squashed lines.
