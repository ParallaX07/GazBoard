## What's Changed

**Notebook paper:** a new Canvas pattern with pale blue lines, a red margin and a clear strip at the top. Each page shows the date it was started, in your language, and keeps it. A "Show the date" switch turns it off.

**The ruling now prints:** grid, lines, dots and notebook paper appear in PNG and PDF exports, the way they look on screen.

**Writing that runs to the edge of a page is simply cut off.** Export no longer asks to "fit everything on" and re-centre your whole page; only things completely off the paper are offered a move.

**No more faint boxes when erasing** on dark boards or notebook paper at 125% or 150% display scaling, and full redraws are faster.
